Workflow · 1:45

AI proposes. The gate decides.

1 FailAction or assertion throws
2 ClassifyNetwork and auth errors are skipped
3 Still in DOM?Yes: timing issue, wait longer, no AI
4 AI proposesSelector + confidence, from up to 30 candidates
5 GateOne match, visible, enabled, same role and name
6 Retry + logSame action or assertion

Defect vs drift · 6:00

Heal the locator.
Never the expectation.

DriftReplacement already contains the expected text. Test goes green.
DefectText missing. Gate rejects. Original error is thrown. Test stays red.

AI plus heuristics. A reviewer owns the final call.
